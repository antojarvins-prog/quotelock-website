7-Day Done-For-You Setup

How QuoteLock Turns Missed Calls & Cold Quotes Into Booked Inspections.

Zero DIY building. We register your +61 number, write your Aussie SMS templates, and load your old quotes in one week.

10-Second Missed-Call Text-Back

When you’re on a roof and miss a call, QuoteLock fires an instant SMS from your +61 number before the homeowner dials the next contractor on Google.

  • Captures homeowner suburb & job scope automatically
  • Stops prospects calling 3 competitors
  • All replies land inside one clean mobile app inbox
Example Automated SMS (Sent in 9s)

“G’day, sorry I missed your call — I’m up on a roof at the moment! Are you looking for a repair, replacement, or quote? Flick back your suburb and I’ll give you a bell as soon as I’m down. — Dave, Apex Roofing”

14-Day Automated Quote Follow-Up

Friendly, human-sounding SMS and email nudges check in with homeowners over 14 days until they book in or give you an answer.

  • Day 2: Quick SMS checking they received the quote
  • Day 5: Friendly check-in on materials & timeline
  • Day 10 & 14: Schedule nudge before next month fills up
Day 5 Automated Check-In

“Hey Sarah, just checking in on that Colorbond re-roof quote I sent over Tuesday. Any questions on the guttering or timeline? Happy to tweak options if needed. — Dave”

Old Quote Reactivation (Instant Cash Flow)

In Week 2, we upload your unclosed estimates from the past 12 months and send a targeted campaign—typically booking 2 to 5 extra inspections in week one.

  • Zero extra ad spend required
  • Turns forgotten spreadsheets into booked inspections
  • Re-runs quarterly or monthly depending on your plan
Week 2 Database Reactivation SMS

“G’day Mark, Dave from Apex Roofing here. We quoted your roof restoration a few months back — just checking if you ever got that sorted or still wanted us to take a look?”

1-Tap Google Review Booster

Tap one button on your mobile app at job handover to text the homeowner a direct link to leave a 5-star Google review.

  • 1 tap on your phone right at handover
  • Direct deep-link straight to your Google review box
  • Pushes your Google Business Profile up local search
Post-Job 1-Tap Review SMS

“Thanks for having the Apex team out today, Mark! If you’ve got 20 seconds, a quick Google review helps our local crew heaps: [Direct Google Link]”
